Our Story

How Hope Revival Center began.

In 2006, a small group of seven teenagers came together, united by one deep hunger and thirst: to know God, encounter His presence, and see Him move in our generation.

We began gathering in a prayer room, seeking God almost every day. Every Saturday, we would come together for our cell group, worshipping, praying, studying the Word, and crying out for a greater move of God.

As we continued to seek Him, God began to speak to us. He placed promises in our hearts and revealed His purposes for our lives and for our nation.

Then, on April 10, 2007, we received a defining word from God: He had called us to ignite revival in our country and ultimately see that revival reach the nations of the world.

That moment marked the beginning of a journey — a journey of revival that first began within our own hearts and within our small cell group.

As we continued to pursue God, we began witnessing miracles, signs, and wonders. Hearts were transformed, lives were touched, and a deep spiritual hunger began to spread.

A fire was ignited.

What began with seven teenagers in a prayer room became the beginning of a movement. And that fire, by the grace of God, is still burning today.

Our story is a testimony that revival can begin with a few people who are willing to seek God wholeheartedly.

Seven teenagers. One prayer room.
One divine calling. One fire.

And the fire is still burning.

Our Vision

Our Mission

Igniting revival, restoring hope.

To ignite revival, restore hope, transform families, and raise a generation of purpose-driven revival agents who will impact the Church, communities, and nations for Christ.

We Exist To

Five commitments
that guide us.

Everything we do flows out of these callings.

Church

Restore Hope in the Church

Rekindle faith, renew hearts, and awaken the Church to its identity and calling in Christ.

Agents

Raise Revival Agents

Equip and mobilize men and women who will carry the fire of revival into churches, communities, nations, and every sphere of society.

Be a Hub of Revival Catalysts

Create a spiritual center where people are awakened, equipped, connected, and sent to ignite revival wherever God has called them.
